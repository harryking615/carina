Carina Renee

Autumn Sunrise Healing

Carina Renee is a Reiki Practitioner, Intuitive Healer,
and the founder of Autumn Sunrise Healing, a space created to help
people reconnect with themselves, heal from within, and
step more fully into who they were created to be.

Her approach is deeply intuitive and centered
on the belief that healing is not about becoming someone new
— it’s about reconnecting with the authentic self that has been there all along.
Through intuitive healing, energy work, sound, breath, and guided self-exploration,
Carina creates a safe, welcoming space for people to pause, release
what no longer serves them, and come back to themselves.

Her own journey of transformation has shaped the way
she holds space for others: with compassion, curiosity, authenticity,
and a belief that even the hardest seasons can become
part of our healing and growth.

Her intention is simple: to help people
reconnect, heal, live whole, and step into who they were created to be.
